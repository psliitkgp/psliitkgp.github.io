## Research Intrests
- Light manipulation in photonic cavities and Purcell enhanced laser action through Tamm modes of photonic crystals.
- Time resolved spectroscopy studies of Rare-earth doped systems, both in visible and IR region.
- Radiative decay engineering of emitters in presence of metal and dielectric nanoparticles in a defect incorporated photonic crystal structures and its application in sensing.
