I am an M.Tech student in Functional Materials and Devices at IIT Kharagpur, with an academic foundation built through my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Physics from the University of Allahabad. 

My research journey has given me hands-on experience in making materials and understanding their real-world behavior—whether it was synthesizing graphene films for electronic devices during my time at CSIR-CSIO and working on broadband antireflection coatings for solar cells as part of my current project. I am also involved in the Photonics Systems Lab under the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur, where I explore optical materials and learn how small changes in processing can shape device performance. I’m motivated by curiosity, practical learning, and the desire to grow through meaningful research.
