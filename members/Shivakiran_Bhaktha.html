Micro-Nano Photonics Lab

Shivakiran Bhaktha B. N. received his Masters of Science (Physics) from Sri Sathya Sai Institute of Higher Learning, Prasanthi Nilayam, India in 2003, and Ph.D. under a co-tutored program from the School of Physics, University of Hyderabad, India and University of Trento, Italy, in 2006. He is currently a Professor in the Department of Physics, Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, India and is also an associate member of the School of Nano Science and Technology at Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. His group comprising of motivated young researchers work on a variety of fundamental and applied research problems in the field of photonics.

Research Interests

Random Lasers, Photonic crystals, Microcavities, Optomechanics, Glass Photonics, Optofluidics and Nonlinear Optics.
